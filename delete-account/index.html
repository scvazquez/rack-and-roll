Delete your Rack & Roll account

Rack & Roll (com.rackandroll.app) · Effective 2026-11-20

You can delete your account and all cloud data from inside the app. It takes a minute and nobody has to approve it.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Rack & Roll and make sure you are signed in.
  2. Open Settings: tap the gear icon at the top of the Plans, Progress or Coach screen, or tap your avatar on Home.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my data.
  4. Tap Delete on the first prompt.
  5. On the second prompt choose Delete cloud account and data.

The second prompt also offers Remove local data only. That clears this phone and leaves your cloud account untouched, so it does not delete your account.

Cloud deletion starts after a 60-second grace period so any upload in progress can finish. The app wipes the phone right away. If the server needs longer, the app says deletion is pending and offers Check status. The server keeps retrying until it finishes.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in identity (the account itself).
  • Synced workout data: sessions, sets, plans, exercise notes and stats, bodyweight log, gym profiles, key lifts, increments and exercise preferences.
  • Body-composition scans and their revisions, saved fitness context, and health evidence settings and permissions.
  • AI coach reports and coach action records.
  • Body-scan files uploaded for import.
  • Your Bandmates data: profile, alias, avatar, memberships and contributions.
  • Everything stored on the phone, when you use the in-app option.

What is kept, and for how long

  • A deletion receipt (a request ID and status, with no workout or health content) is kept for about 30 days after completion so the app can confirm the deletion. Then it is removed.
  • Data we sent to Google's Gemini API for coach or scan requests is governed by Google's own terms. We cannot delete it from Google's side.
  • Health Connect data stays in Android's Health Connect on your phone. Remove it there.
  • Other band members may have seen your alias and stats before you deleted. We remove your records from our servers, not their memory.
  • If you connected WHOOP or Oura, also remove Rack & Roll's access in those services.
